The pattern archive

Patterns, cut to you.

Eleven classic garments, from a T-shirt to a men's shirt and a tailored jacket, drafted live from your measurements with the tailor's block method. Begin one, redraw it on the cutting table, sew it in the fitting room, then follow the making book.

Your own

Bring your own pattern

Import an SVG from FreeSewing, Seamly2D or Inkscape, or trace a photo of a paper pattern. Redraw it, sew the seams in 3D and get a making book.

Where the patterns come from

FreeSewing

An open-source (MIT) platform with dozens of made-to-measure designs, from body blocks (Brian, Bella, Breanna) to Simon, Jaeger, Wahid and Titan. Export an SVG there and import it here to redraw it.

freesewing.eu/designs

Seamly2D

Free, GPLv3 parametric pattern-drafting software for Windows, macOS and Linux. Draft blocks from measurement files, export SVG, bring them here for 3D.

github.com/FashionFreedom/Seamly2D

The tailoring manuals

Victorian cutting systems such as W. D. F. Vincent's Cutters' Practical Guide (1890s) taught the block method these drafts follow: draft a block from measurements, then redraw it.
