Questions at the counter

Do I need to know how to draft patterns?

No. Every pattern is drafted for you from your measurements with the classic tailor's block method. Redrawing is optional, and the making book explains every step.

Is it free?

Yes. Drafting, the 3D fitting, fit changes, printing and the making book are free. If a tailor makes a garment for you, you agree the price with them and pay them directly.

How accurate is the 3D fitting?

It is a real cloth simulation (stretch, bending, gravity, friction) on a mannequin built from your numbers, which makes it great for seeing how pieces join and where a garment is tight or loose. It is not a substitute for a toile: always sew a test in cheap calico before cutting good cloth.

What do I do after my toile?

Open the garment in the Studio, choose Fit changes, and type what it needed, for example "take in 1 cm at the side, let out the hips half an inch, shorten the sleeves 2 cm". We show how each change is read before anything is redrafted.

How does the commission board work?

You post a request from your garment's docket: the cloth, the size, your budget and city, never your name or measurements. Seamstresses and tailors send quotes, which the shop reviews before you see them. Accept one, and only then does that tailor see your full measurements. You decide when to share your email.

Where do the patterns come from?

Our own code drafts them, after the block method in classic tailoring manuals such as Vincent's Cutters' Practical Guide and open-source projects like FreeSewing (MIT) and Seamly2D (GPLv3). You can import patterns from those tools too.

How do I print a pattern?

From the Studio's lay plan: tiled A4 or US Letter pages at true scale with a 5 cm test square and a page map, an SVG for a copy shop, or projector mode for your cutting mat.

Who sees my measurements?

Only you, and a tailor whose quote you accept. They stay in your browser, and on your account if you open one. Garments are private unless you share them in the showroom. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.
